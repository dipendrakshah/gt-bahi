# 05 — Front-door competitors

“Front door” = the surface that captures demand and creates an order. Bahi is not trying to be the only front door. Bahi needs front doors to *write into* it.

## Blanxer

| | |
|---|---|
| Entity | Blanxer Technology Pvt. Ltd., Lokanthali-1, Bhaktapur |
| Founded | 2022–2023 |
| People | Prashant Phuyal (CEO), Kiran Neupane (CTO); team ~11–50, closer to 15 |
| Funding | Unfunded / bootstrapped in public profiles |
| Pitch | Commerce OS: site + QR checkout + inventory + logistics + POS |

**Why they matter to Bahi**

They are the most credible owned-storefront company in Nepal. They already sit on Fonepay dynamic QR and 3PL APIs. They do not sit on a serious ledger. Their Tigg integration is the exact seam Bahi should either *become* or *replace*.

**Coexist strategy:** Woo-style plugin / official “post orders and payments to Bahi.” Do not start a theme war.

**Risk:** they build good enough books. Watch whether they hire an IRD specialist or acquire a billing vendor.

## Zalient Shop

Free builder, late-2025 launch, NFC-card company adjacent. Chat-to-order narrative. Distribution is early. Treat as a future API customer if they survive, not as a threat to the ledger.

## Meta (chat surface)

Meta Business Agent (announced globally mid-2026) answers DMs, recommends catalog items, books, and aims to close sales on WhatsApp / IG / Messenger. Pricing experiments in the $38–50/mo band globally after a free period.

**Bahi does not out-converse Meta.** Bahi is the inventory and tax brain Meta cannot see unless the merchant connects it. Year-2 work: a connector so Meta’s agent (or a merchant-owned agent) is only allowed to promise what Bahi says is true.

## Accounting vendors as competitors *and* channels

Tigg, OneFlow, Lekhapal, MISAC, BUSY, Tally. On day one they are competitors for the “books” budget. On day two a CA might run Bahi *and* Tally. Do not require religious conversion in month 1. Offer:

- Bahi as operational subledger + export to Tally
- Or Bahi as system of record replacing Tally for new VAT businesses

Replacement is the long-term goal. Export is the foot in the door.

## Payment vendors

F1Soft and NCHL are infrastructure. Partner. Never posture as a wallet.
