# Research notes and sources

Research was performed 18 September 2026 during the originating chat. Figures below are **snapshots**, many from vendor marketing. Re-verify before using in a pitch to a CA or an investor.

## Americas stack

- Public SaaS league tables and 2026 commentary: Salesforce as large standalone application SaaS; Microsoft/Google as usage leaders in collaboration; Intuit, Shopify, ServiceNow, Workday, Stripe/Square in their layers.
- Vertical SaaS examples widely cited: Toast (restaurants), ServiceTitan (trades), Procore (construction), Veeva (life sciences), Clio (legal).

## Nepal payments

- eSewa / F1Soft group reporting and journalism on user and merchant network scale.
- Fonepay as QR/IBFT pipe; commentary that it handles a large share of QR.
- NCHL ConnectIPS / NEPALPAY as high-value and institutional rail; claims of large share of digital *value*.
- NRB-linked indicators cited in 2026 articles: wallet account and mobile-banking counts above population; QR merchant value growth from tens of billions NPR to ~500B class between FY 2021/22 and 2023/24; dozens of PSP/PSO licenses.
- Khalti–IME Pay 2025 combination in market commentary.

## Nepal accounting / IRD

- Market lists naming TallyPrime, Swastik, Tigg, OneFlow, Lekhapal, BISage, MISAC, BUSY, ERPNext Nepal packs.
- Cloud price band commonly advertised NPR 6k–32k/year.
- QuickBooks gaps: no native IRD books, CBMS, BS FY, Nepal payroll.
- Computerized billing / CBMS thresholds and behaviors as described on vendor pages and news (verify against IRD).
- 2026 reporting on a possible state-owned e-billing client.

## MCP / agents (global 2026)

- MCP donated to foundation governance; large SDK download figures in industry blogs.

## Economics context

- Nepal SMB software is yearly-NPR priced, not $39/seat.
- Implementation and CA labor dominate switching cost, not UI.
