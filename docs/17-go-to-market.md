# 17 — Go to market

## Channel stack (in order)

1. **Founder network of D2C operators** — people already paying Blanxer or running Woo
2. **Chartered accountants** — one trusted CA is worth 20 cold ads
3. **Seller groups** — Facebook groups for Instagram sellers, TikTok seller chats
4. **Agencies** who build Woo shops
5. Paid ads — only after a landing page with a NPR price and a 15-minute demo calendar

## Narrative by audience

**Founder:**  
“Your QR money and your VAT bill will finally be the same story.”

**Operator:**  
“DM se order, stock reserve, bill nikalne — ek thau.”

**CA:**  
“You get a live unmatched list and IRD-shaped books without visiting their shop.”

**Developer:**  
“Orders and invoices over a real API. MCP for the tools you already use.”

Never lead with MCP in a merchant meeting.

## Sales process

1. 20-minute pain call: how do they invoice today, which rails, who is the CA
2. Look at one messy week of QR + COD (screenshare)
3. Pilot: last 7 days of orders entered, matcher on new QR
4. Quote Operate + implementation if branches > 1
5. CA introduced in week 2 of pilot, not week 8

Cycle time target: 21 days from first call to paid. Expect 45.

## Onboarding

- Catalog import CSV
- Opening stock
- Opening unmatched (optional)
- Invoice serial start agreed with CA
- One rail first (Fonepay), then wallets

If onboarding > 8 hours of our time without an implementation fee, we are doing agency work.

## Content that is worth writing

- “Why QuickBooks fails IRD”
- “How to reconcile Fonepay with invoices”
- “What CBMS will reject”
- A public match-rate dashboard for our own demo shop

Content that is not worth writing: “Top 10 AI tools for Nepali CEOs.”

## Partnerships

- Seek *technical* partnership with one rail and one cloud accounting vendor, not logos on a slide
- Woo agencies: 20% of first-year subscription
- Do not partner exclusively with Blanxer in a way that blocks Woo

## Brand

Quiet, CA-safe, bilingual fiscal documents. The marketing site can be sharp. The invoice PDF should look like a document an IRD officer has seen before, not a startup drip page.
