# ADR 0004 — First ICP is the D2C operator

- Status: accepted
- Date: 2026-09-18

## Context

Temptations: restaurants (loud POS market), kirana (huge count, tiny ARPU), exporters, hospitals.

## Decision

Year-1 design partners and paid customers are VAT-oriented multi-channel sellers (web + social ± one counter), GMV roughly NPR 2–20M/month.

## Consequences

We will refuse restaurant pilots. We will look slow on “SMEs in Nepal” slides. ARPU can actually work.
