# 04 — Nepal layer map (snapshot September 2026)

## Sequence of digitization

1. Mobile wallets (eSewa from 2009)
2. Bank apps + Fonepay QR + ConnectIPS
3. Marketplaces (Daraz) and Facebook commerce
4. IRD computerized billing / CBMS
5. Cloud accounting and HRMS
6. Owned storefronts (Blanxer pack) — current inning
7. Agent interfaces — just starting

Bahi lives between 4, 5, 6, and 7.

## Payments (most mature homegrown layer)

**Wallets:** eSewa (F1Soft) remains the default consumer brand; company reports in 2026 discourse cite ~10M+ users and very large merchant networks. Khalti merged with IME Pay (2025) into a broader financial surface. Prabhu Pay and others on remittance corridors.

**Pipes:** Fonepay (F1Soft) is the QR / IBFT fabric behind bank apps — the Square-network analog. NCHL ConnectIPS / NEPALPAY is the other national rail, strong on high-value, tax, rent, school fees. QR vs QR split (Fonepay vs NEPALPAY) is a live interoperability issue.

**NRB-scale color:** wallet accounts and mobile-banking users exceed population; QR merchant value jumped by orders of magnitude from FY 2021/22 to 2023/24. Dozens of PSPs/PSOs licensed. Margins on consumer wallets are thin; pipes and take-rate matter more than app UI.

**Implication for Bahi:** never become a PSP in year one. Integrate all four of eSewa, Khalti, Fonepay, ConnectIPS. Win on *matching* and *posting*, not on acquiring.

## Commerce

- **Daraz** (Alibaba): traffic and logistics giant. Commission 8–15% class. Customer file stays with Daraz.
- **Hamrobazar:** classifieds.
- **SastoDeal, Gyapu, Neshop:** challengers on fashion, groceries, zero-commission narratives.
- **Independent stores:** WooCommerce still leads verified store counts; Shopify present but small for domestic NPR checkout. Custom carts common.
- **Real volume:** Facebook / Instagram / TikTok + COD + QR.

## Books

- Floor: **TallyPrime Nepal** + **Swastik** (trading/inventory).
- Cloud Nepal-first: **Tigg, OneFlow, Lekhapal, BISage, MISAC, evieBooks, Tshul, Nepal E-Billing**, plus **BUSY** localized.
- Open source: ERPNext + Nepal Compliance apps.
- Price band SMB cloud: roughly **NPR 6,000–32,000 per year**.
- Global QuickBooks: workaround city. No native CBMS, BS year, TDS headings.

## People

RigoHR incumbent. Pace HRMS, Kumari Job, NepalHRM and others. International Gusto/Rippling/Deel do not compute SSF/PF/CIT/eTDS/Dashain/Labour Act 2074. Bahi should **not** build full HRMS in year 1–2. Payslip export / salary journal only if a beachhead customer demands it.

## Workspace

Google Workspace and Microsoft 365. Do not rebuild.

## Vertical

Restaurant POS is the crowded vertical (RestroHub, Restronp, Hamro SAN, NRestro, Cafe OS, maBill, Pahuna, Saauzi restaurant modes). Hotels have local PMS. Home services / construction / legal / clinics are Excel. Bahi does not enter restaurants as primary ICP.

## IT sector backdrop

Nepal exports software services at meaningful scale (IIDS-era figures already hundreds of millions USD; industry commentary in 2025–26 talks about crossing ~$1B with messy remittance classification). Government “Decade of IT” rhetoric is real; domestic SMB product companies are still small. Talent can be hired. Distribution cannot.

## Structural implication

Payments and tax created *forced* software markets. Commerce OS is being built by Blanxer-class companies. CRM never happened. The connective tissue between forced tax software and new storefronts is unpaid labor by 24-year-old founders and their CAs. That labor is Bahi’s TAM.
