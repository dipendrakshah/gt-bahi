# 03 — Americas reference stack

This is the map that started the conversation. Nepal strategy is a *translation*, not a copy.

## How value concentrates

In North America, the expensive companies own a **system of record** and then tax the flows that pass through it.

| Record | Flow they tax later |
|---|---|
| Store (Shopify) | Payments, capital, apps |
| POS (Toast, Square) | Card acquiring |
| Books (Intuit) | Payroll, Mailchimp, tax filing |
| CRM (Salesforce) | Data cloud, automation, Slack |
| Employee (Rippling / Workday) | Devices, spend, global payroll |
| Job site (Procore, ServiceTitan) | Payments, financing, adjacent modules |

## Layer detail (compressed)

### Commerce
Shopify is the default serious D2C OS. WooCommerce wins on fee-avoidance. BigCommerce on high-volume catalogs. Square Online when the merchant already has a Square brick.

### Payments
Stripe for internet-native and subscriptions. Square for local + hardware. Shopify Payments to avoid the platform’s third-party surcharge (Stripe on the back). PayPal for buyer trust. Toast for restaurants.

### Accounting
QuickBooks Online is the US SMB standard because of accountants. Xero for unlimited users / international. FreshBooks for invoicing-heavy services. Wave/Zoho at the bottom. NetSuite / Sage Intacct when you outgrow SMB GL.

### CRM and marketing
Salesforce for enterprise. HubSpot for SMB inbound. Klaviyo for ecommerce email/SMS, especially Shopify shops.

### People
Rippling as HR+IT+payroll unifier. Gusto for friendly SMB payroll. ADP for scale. Workday for enterprise HCM/finance. Deel for international contractors.

### Workspace
Microsoft 365 vs Google Workspace. Slack, Zoom, Atlassian, Asana/monday/ClickUp.

### Vertical OS
Toast, ServiceTitan, Procore, Clio, Veeva, AppFolio, Motive. Often more valuable than horizontal tools because they own a daily workflow *and* payments.

## Translation rules for Nepal

Copy the *pattern*, not the brand:

1. Own a record the merchant cannot leave without pain.
2. Attach money movement or compliance to that record.
3. Sell through the profession that already holds trust (US: CPAs; Nepal: CAs + seller-group admins).
4. Do not start as a suite. Shopify did not launch Capital on day one. Intuit did not launch Mailchimp on day one.

Bahi’s copied pattern: **QuickBooks’ accountant lock-in + Shopify’s order object + Stripe’s API surface**, localized to IRD + Fonepay.
