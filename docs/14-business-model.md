# 14 — Business model

## Principle

Charge for **removing a clerk and de-risking IRD**, not for “AI.” Attach a small fee to **matched digital money** only when the matcher is doing real work.

## Packaged offers

### Bahi Start — NPR 2,000 / month or NPR 20,000 / year

- 1 branch, 1 warehouse
- 3 users
- Manual + CSV payments
- Invoices + sales book
- Email support
- No CBMS, no agents, no API

Foot in the door for pre-VAT or tiny VAT.

### Bahi Operate — NPR 4,500 / month or NPR 48,000 / year

- 3 branches
- 10 users
- Payment rails + matcher
- Woo / widget
- API
- SMS pack extra
- CBMS if/when certified
- Suggest-mode agent

**Default target package.**

### Bahi Firm — NPR 8,000 / month + implementation

- Multi-branch
- CA portal included for 1 firm
- MCP enabled
- Priority support
- Custom rail mapping

### CA desk — NPR 800 / client / month billed to the firm

Volume discount at 20+ clients. Read + export + unmatched queue. Write limited to comments and match suggestions.

## Attach

| Attach | Rate | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| Matched digital payments | 0.4% | Cap NPR 8,000/month in year 1 so we are not Daraz |
| Or per successful match | NPR 3 | Alternative if % feels political |
| WhatsApp conversation | pass-through + 15% | Never hide Meta’s bill |
| Implementation | NPR 15,000–40,000 | Required above 2 branches |
| Extra SMS | prepaid packs | |

Do not take a second cut on Fonepay MDR. Merchants already pay the rail. Our cut is for *reconciliation*, advertised as such.

## What we will not do

- Per-SKU fees
- Per-invoice fees that punish volume
- USD list prices
- Lifetime deals that lock FY2082 prices forever (Tigg-style lifetime is a trap for us)

## Expansion revenue (year 3+, not year 1 plan)

- Purchases / AP
- Multi-entity groups
- White-label API for other front doors
- Credit-decision data *if* a licensed partner underwrites — we do not become a lender

## Sales motion

Founder-led + CA referrals + seller Facebook groups. No SDR team until 80 paying Operate orgs.

## Packaging psychology

Tally buyers think in years. D2C operators think in months. Always show both. Always show “vs one junior’s salary.”
