# 18 — Risks and kill criteria

## Market risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Blanxer builds good-enough books | Seam disappears | Become their ledger via API faster than they build GL |
| Tigg/OneFlow add order+matcher | They have CA trust | Win on live channels + agents; consider partnership |
| Government free e-billing | UI commodity | Moat = source documents + matcher, not PDF |
| Meta agent + catalog | Chat commodity | We remain truth source; integrate |
| Daraz keeps demand | Harder D2C ICP | Focus merchants who already left or dual-track |
| Price war at NPR 6k/year | ARPU collapse | Refuse kirana ICP; sell Operate to operators |

## Compliance risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Never listed for computerized billing | Cannot sell to obligated firms | Bridge ADR early; do not advertise approval |
| Wrong serial / editable invoice | CA walkout, legal heat | Immutability tests; advisor sign-off |
| Thresholds change | Scope shift | Configurable policies |
| Data residency / leakage via MCP | Trust death | Tenant isolation + tool audit + deny-by-default issue |

## Product risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |

| Agent writes bad invoices | IRD | Default deny issue tool |

## Company risks

| Risk | Why it matters | Mitigation |
|---|---|---|
| Burn at 10 people too early | Cash out | Headcount freeze rules in `15` |
| Founder cannot sell | Velocity | ICP interviews week 1; if 20 talks yield 0 pilots, change message |
| Key person CA advisor leaves | Quality | Written numbering spec |
| Talent pulled to export IT salaries | Slow build | Equity + scope discipline |

## Kill criteria (pre-agreed)

Stop or hard-pivot if any of these hit:

1. Month 6: zero design partners using issued invoices as their *real* bills
2. Month 12: fewer than 15 organizations sending weekly production orders
3. Month 18: fewer than 40 paying, ARPU under NPR 3,000
4. Any fiscal incident we cannot reconstruct from the audit log
5. We spend two consecutive months on theme/chat work while matcher queue age rises

Killing is a success if it saves 12 months of pretend.

## Risks we accept

- Small TAM versus US SaaS (we price and staff for Nepal)
- Slow CA sales cycles
- Rail APIs that are worse than Stripe
- Having to say no to restaurants
